## Decisions made along the way

- **ADR-0012: the compiled port is 32-bit (i686).** `archive.c:Locate`
  relocates pointers into u32 slots and HSD descriptors store 4-byte pointers;
  a 64-bit build truncates. `-m32` is what made S0b work; ACGC uses the same.
- **P-601 shim hardening:** 1021/1034 `src/*.c` compile; `shim/` carries
  `decomp_shim.h` (`ssize_t`, `<stdint.h>`), `Runtime/platform.h`
  (`STATIC_ASSERT` neutralised), `stdbool.h` (`bool` = `int`).
