> slowdown, but Link's leg matrices still went NaN and the frame-720 capture

> unswapped, so `ft_80089B08`/`lbBgFlash_80021410` computed NaN angles. Fixed

## Root cause

The slowdown was not a changed vertex format, color decoder or NBT path.
Temporary capture counters found tens of thousands of non-finite position and
normal-matrix components during the bad interval.  A conditional GDB trace
followed the first broken Link joint through `SetupEnvelopeModelMtx` to
`lbBgFlash_80021410` foot/leg IK.  Its angle was NaN because the upstream host
placeholder maps PowerPC `__frsqrte` to `sqrt`, while every caller expects a
reciprocal-square-root estimate and performs Newton-Raphson refinement.

Those NaN matrices make the 32-bit x87 transform loop drastically slower.  At
frame 720 the HLE handled 40,239 non-finite matrix components and 23,040
non-finite transform outputs with the same 61,821 captured vertices as frame
660.  The NaN *source* was not fully fixed here: a second NaN producer
(unswapped `ftData->x58` IK lengths, see the correction above) still fed
`fn_8002113C` NaN angles after this commit, so the slowdown was gone but the
legs stayed broken until P-627.

Correcting the intrinsic exposed a second host-FP problem at initial fighter
animation blending.  GCC's 32-bit x87 backend kept a `float` quaternion dot
product in 80-bit precision across its branch, then rounded to 1.0 only at the
`acosf` call.  This selected a zero-angle spherical interpolation and divided
by `sin(0)`.  The 32-bit targets now use SSE2 scalar FP, matching declared
float/double evaluation widths and avoiding the slower x87 path.

## Changes

- Added `native/decomp/shim/placeholder.h`, which includes the decomp header
  then supplies correct reciprocal-square-root semantics.
- Added `-msse2 -mfpmath=sse` to every 32-bit decomp target.
- Added direct regressions for `__frsqrte(4) == 0.5` and the exact adjacent
  quaternion pair that previously became NaN.
- Repaired the P-628 channel-state direct test, which still referenced the
  removed CPU-side `GxHleVertex.ras` member and prevented a clean full build.
- The match pacer clears stale sleep metrics and immediately re-anchors after
  any missed deadline instead of allowing up to two frames of burst catch-up.

## Follow-up

P-629 is closed.  P-627 (Link's legs) is **not** closed here — it was fixed in
the follow-up `2026-09-12-P-627-ftdata-x58.md` by converting the
`ftData_x58_t` big-endian floats in `hsd_convert.c` (v58).  The remaining
CPU-side vertex transform pipeline can still be moved to a GL matrix palette
later, but that is an architectural optimization rather than a fix for this
incident.
