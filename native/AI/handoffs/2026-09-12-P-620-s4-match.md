## What I tried that did not work

- **Repacking the script words with a byte swap** (and the sorted-pointer
  interval walk).  A byte swap maps the opcode to bits 24..29, not 0..5, and
  the pointer intervals bridge unrelated data (WaitAnimData arrays, attrs),
  double-converting fields.  Reverted in favour of `scalar_storage_order`.
- **`scalar_storage_order` on `Fighter`/`x594_s32`**: the `x594` union has
  byte-view and word-view overlays whose fields conflict under GCC's LSB-first
  bitfields; only the `x597_bits`/`x594_bits` word view is repacked (see
  `conv_waitanim_flags`), which is enough for the part-vis masks.
- **Per-pair `conv_u16` without `pair_count * 2`**: only 10.5 pairs converted;
  the rest stayed big-endian.
