## What I did

- Compiled the decomp's `src/sysdolphin/baselib/axdriver.c` and the SDK's
  pure-C AX layer (`extern/dolphin/src/dolphin/ax/` + `axfx/axfx.c` and
  `delay.c`), deleting every AXDriver/AX/AI stub.  `AXOut.c` is replaced by
  `native/audio/ax_hle.c` (exact frame order, VI-derived 200 Hz pump).
- Added `native/audio/ax_mixer.c`: DSP-ADPCM (8-byte/14-sample frames),
  PCM 10/25, 16.16 ratio SRC, `AXPBMIX` main/aux routing, VE ramps, ITD,
  loop/end/current-address write-back, voice repositioning resync.
- Ported `reverb_std`'s asm `HandleReverb` to C in
  `native/decomp/axfx/axfx_port.c`; `reverb_hi`/`chorus` report init failure
  (Melee never registers them).
- Converters: `platform/ssm.c` (header + u16 entry fields, address Hi/Lo
  pairs, chunked reads), `platform/sem.c` (5 sections + command-stream
  words), `platform/hps.c` (stream header, page tables).  DVD detects them.
- Endianness patches under `PORT_PC` in `synth.c` for the engine's
  `*(u32*) &hi` aliasing (address pairs, SRC ratio); helpers live in
  `decomp_shim.h`; patch list in `learnings/decomp_port.md`.
- Output: `melee_decomp_boot --audio-dump out.wav` (deterministic WAV + hash),
  SDL3 stream in the viewer, `ctest decomp_audio`.
- Fixed four integration bugs (G-097..G-100), the loudest one being the
  DSP-ADPCM frame geometry: 8 bytes/14 samples, scale = low nibble.

## What I tried that did not work

- 9-byte/16-sample ADPCM frames with `scale = header >> 4` (the "standard
  Nintendo" codec): every voice became loud noise.  GC DSP-ADPCM is 8-byte
  frames; `pred_scale` matching the first header byte made the wrong reading
  look plausible.
- Treating `.ssm` loop/end/current as host u32s: Hi/Lo swapped, all SFX
  played from garbage and were silent.  They are two u16 pairs.
- Swapping a `.ssm` u32 before reading `header_size`/`group_count`: `dir_end`
  was byte-swapped and the walk corrupted sample data (`mario.ssm`).
- Pumping completions from `advance_ticks` unconditionally: violates the
  interrupt model (G-063); only `boot_platform_idle_tick` does it, guarded.
- Reusing one destination buffer for two `ssm_fix_read` calls in a test: the
  window-relative writes land in the wrong place.  Tests must model the DVD
  backend's per-read buffers (the real converter is buffer-relative).
