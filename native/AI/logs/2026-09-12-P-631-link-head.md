# P-631 Link head/cap rogue polygon — investigation state

Owner report (2026-09-12): a green polygon "goes rogue" near Link's head in
the live match; visible before and after the P-627 `ftData->x58` fix. This log
records what was ruled out and the concrete next steps. Task row: P-631.

## Repro

```sh
SDL_VIDEODRIVER=offscreen ./build/native/melee_decomp_viewer \
  --match --frames 718 --shot /tmp/head.bmp
# close-crop Link's head, e.g. ImageMagick:
magick /tmp/head.bmp -crop 170x150+370+270 +repage -resize 600% head.png
```

Stills around frames 716–719 show the green cap folding across the face
(zoom crops `/tmp/h716-zoom.png` .. `/tmp/h719-zoom.png`), but the captures
are ambiguous about whether that is a genuine rogue polygon or the cap's own
animation; the owner sees it at speed.

## Ruled out

- **NaN/P-627 regression.** GDB matrix probe over frames 650–720: 71/71
  finite; `fn_8002113C` NaN-angle probe over 720 frames: 0 hits;
  `--dump-draws 720`: zero non-finite NDC sentinel bounds.
- **Static visibility.** Link still hides 29 DObjs in both static and live
  rendering, matching the correct static viewer.
- **Stretched fighter mesh at 716/718.** A GDB capture dump of
  `frame_verts`/`frame_draws` at frames 716 and 718 (61821 verts / 223 draws
  each) shows no fighter draw with a large triangle (max NDC tri area 0.05)
  and no NaN vertices; the largest inter-frame displacement in a fighter
  draw is 0.03 NDC. So those two frames do not contain a rogue triangle.
- **`ftData->x1C` part animation (P-630).** `ftAnim_ApplyPartAnim` is never
  called over 720 frames, so the unconverted `x1C` counts are latent here,
  not the cause.

## Scan results (frames 600–1200, step 30)

21 full frame captures analyzed for triangles with huge NDC area:

```text
frame  900  draw 74 v=6   tex0=30  max tri area 1892
frame  930  draw 83 v=33  tex0=39  max tri area 23546
frame  990  draw 45 v=6   tex0=26  max tri area 1865
frame 1020  draw 45 v=6   tex0=26  max tri area 1864
frame 1200  draw 71 v=183 tex0=26  max tri area 0.42
```

The 900/930/990/1020 frames render without a visible rogue polygon, so those
giant triangles are clipped/off-screen (likely particle or trail quads whose
one vertex projects far away). The frame-1200 triangle is inside the frustum
(area 0.42 NDC ~ 1/5 of the screen) but the rendered frame looks normal, so it
may be behind the camera or depth-rejected. None is confirmed as the owner's
artifact yet.

## Next steps

1. Rasterize the captured draws (or use `--part N --part-mode hide` around the
   suspect frames) to find which draw paints the head region and whether a
   triangle covers it.
2. Focus on 6-vertex `tex0>=25` quads: they are the only draws with giant
   triangles. Identify their source (fighter effects / part anim / ROBj
   billboard) and whether their matrices come from GX HSD or our HLE.
3. Reproduce against the prototype viewer (`melee --model PlLkNr.dat --view
   --animate`) if a matching clip can be selected; it is the parity oracle.
4. Do not filter triangles at the GX layer; the fix belongs at the data or
   matrix source, as with P-627.
